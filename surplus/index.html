Unclaimed Foreclosure & Tax Deed Surplus Funds in South Florida.

When a Miami-Dade or Broward property sells at auction for more than what was owed, the excess money belongs to the former homeowner or heirs—not the bank or the county. We help verify and file your claim with zero upfront cost.

Important Florida Statutory Notice

You are not required to hire an attorney, surplus recovery company, or agent to claim excess proceeds—you may contact the Miami-Dade Clerk of Courts or Broward County Records, Taxes & Treasury directly. If you choose to assign your claim to us, Florida Statute § 45.033 caps our fee at 12% of the recovered foreclosure surplus, paid only if funds are recovered.

Surplus Funds FAQ

Can I claim surplus funds myself without paying anyone?+

Yes. Under Florida law, you are never required to use an agent or attorney. You can contact the Miami-Dade Clerk of Courts or Broward County directly to file your own claim.

What is the maximum fee allowed under Florida law?+

For foreclosure surplus assignments under Fla. Stat. § 45.033, total compensation to an assignee is strictly capped at 12% of the surplus, with zero upfront fees.
