Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to fix code violations, open permits, or roof issues before selling? +

No. We purchase Miami-Dade and Broward properties 100% as-is. Our title team orders municipal lien and permit searches upfront so you never have to pay out-of-pocket for repairs or contractors.

How do I know the closing is safe and legitimate? +

Every closing is conducted through a licensed Florida title company or real estate closing attorney. Funds are verified in escrow and wired directly to your bank account at closing.

What if I live out of state or inherited a property in probate? +

We regularly work with out-of-state owners and heirs. We coordinate remote online notarization (RON) or mobile notaries, and work alongside Florida probate attorneys whenever needed.

Is there any cost or obligation to request an offer? +

Zero cost and zero obligation. You review our written offer on your own time and decide what makes the most sense for your family and timeline.
